BlockAds Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

BlockAds is a browser extension designed to block advertisements, trackers, and related unwanted content while browsing the web. Protecting user privacy is a core part of how BlockAds is designed.

1. Information BlockAds Processes

BlockAds may locally process limited information about the websites you visit in order to provide its core ad-blocking and tracker-blocking functionality.

This may include:

  • The hostname or domain of the website currently being visited.
  • The current page URL when required for extension functionality.
  • Locally generated statistics about blocked advertisements and trackers.
  • Your BlockAds settings and website-specific protection preferences.

2. How Information Is Used

This information is used solely to provide and improve BlockAds functionality, including:

  • Blocking advertisements.
  • Blocking known tracking requests.
  • Removing or hiding advertising elements from webpages.
  • Applying website-specific protection preferences.
  • Displaying local blocking statistics.

3. Local Data Storage

BlockAds stores extension settings, protection preferences, and blocking statistics locally in your browser using Chrome extension storage.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to BlockAds servers.

4. Browsing Activity

BlockAds processes website information only when necessary to provide its user-facing advertisement and tracker blocking features.

BlockAds does not create a remote browsing history, behavioral profile, or advertising profile based on the websites you visit.

5. Data Sharing

BlockAds does not sell, rent, trade, or transfer your personal information or browsing activity to advertisers, data brokers, analytics companies, or other third parties.

6. Advertising

BlockAds does not use your browsing activity to display targeted advertising and does not inject advertisements into webpages.

7. Analytics and Tracking

BlockAds does not use third-party analytics services to track your browsing activity.

8. Remote Code

BlockAds does not download or execute remote code as part of its browser extension functionality.

9. Data Security

BlockAds is designed to minimize data collection. Extension preferences and statistics are stored locally wherever possible to reduce unnecessary transmission of user information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if BlockAds features or data practices change. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or BlockAds privacy practices, you can contact the developer through the official BlockAds support channels or GitHub repository.
