BlockAds Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

BlockAds is a browser extension designed to block advertisements, trackers, and related unwanted content while browsing the web. Protecting user privacy is a core part of how BlockAds is designed.

1. Information BlockAds Processes

BlockAds may locally process limited information about the websites you visit in order to provide its core ad-blocking and tracker-blocking functionality.

This may include:

  • The hostname or domain of the website currently being visited.
  • The current page URL when required locally for extension functionality.
  • Locally generated statistics about blocked advertisements and trackers.
  • Your BlockAds settings and website-specific protection preferences.

BlockAds does not automatically transmit your browsing history or the full URLs of websites you visit.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by BlockAds is used solely to provide and improve the extension's user-facing functionality, including:

  • Blocking advertisements.
  • Blocking known tracking requests.
  • Removing or hiding advertising elements from webpages.
  • Applying website-specific protection preferences.
  • Displaying local blocking statistics.
  • Investigating problems voluntarily reported by users.

3. Local Data Storage

BlockAds stores extension settings, protection preferences, and blocking statistics locally in your browser using Chrome extension storage.

These locally stored preferences and statistics are not automatically transmitted to the BlockAds feedback system or used to create a remote browsing profile.

4. Voluntary Problem Reports

BlockAds includes an optional problem reporting feature that allows users to voluntarily report websites where advertisements, trackers, or related unwanted content were not blocked correctly.

A problem report is transmitted only when the user explicitly chooses to submit it.

A submitted problem report may contain:

  • The domain of the website where the problem occurred.
  • The problem description entered by the user.
  • The installed BlockAds extension version.
  • The date and time when the report was received.

BlockAds does not intentionally include the full page URL, browsing history, account information, name, email address, or other directly identifying information in these reports.

Users should avoid entering personal or sensitive information in the problem description field.

Problem reports are used solely to investigate reported issues and improve BlockAds advertisement and tracker blocking functionality.

5. Feedback Service

Voluntary problem reports are transmitted using Google Apps Script and stored using Google Sheets infrastructure controlled by the BlockAds developer.

Google may process technical information necessary to operate these services in accordance with Google's own privacy and security practices.

BlockAds does not use submitted problem reports for advertising, behavioral profiling, cross-site tracking, or sale to third parties.

6. Browsing Activity

BlockAds processes website information only when necessary to provide its user-facing advertisement and tracker blocking functionality.

BlockAds does not create or maintain a remote browsing history, behavioral profile, or advertising profile based on the websites you visit.

A website domain is transmitted to the feedback service only when the user voluntarily submits a problem report through BlockAds.

Normal browsing does not cause BlockAds to automatically transmit visited website domains to the feedback service.

7. Data Sharing

BlockAds does not sell, rent, trade, or disclose browsing activity or personal information to advertisers, data brokers, or analytics companies.

Information voluntarily submitted through the problem reporting feature is processed using Google infrastructure solely for receiving, storing, reviewing, and investigating those reports.

8. Advertising

BlockAds does not use your browsing activity to display targeted advertising and does not inject advertisements into webpages.

9. Analytics and Tracking

BlockAds does not use third-party analytics services to monitor or profile your browsing activity.

BlockAds does not use submitted problem reports for analytics-based advertising, behavioral profiling, or cross-site tracking.

10. Remote Code

BlockAds does not download or execute remote code as part of its browser extension functionality.

Communication with the BlockAds feedback service is used only to transmit voluntary problem reports and does not download executable extension code.

11. Data Security

BlockAds is designed to minimize the amount of user information processed or transmitted. Extension preferences and blocking statistics are stored locally wherever possible.

Information transmitted through the optional problem reporting feature is limited to the information reasonably necessary to investigate reported blocking problems.

BlockAds does not intentionally collect passwords, payment information, authentication credentials, or other sensitive account information.

12. Data Retention

Local BlockAds settings and statistics remain in Chrome extension storage until they are changed, cleared, or the extension is removed, subject to Chrome's storage behavior.

Voluntary problem reports may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to investigate reported issues, improve BlockAds, and maintain the reliability of its blocking functionality.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if BlockAds features, infrastructure, or data practices change.

Any changes will be published on this page together with an updated revision date.

14. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or BlockAds privacy practices, you can contact the developer through the official BlockAds support channels or GitHub repository.
